**Data sources and reuse**

**Source: ECB statistics.** The observations come from the European Central Bank (ECB), series [`EXR.D.USD.EUR.SP00.A`](https://data.ecb.europa.eu/data/datasets/EXR/EXR.D.USD.EUR.SP00.A), obtained through the [ECB data API](https://data-api.ecb.europa.eu/service/data/EXR/D.USD.EUR.SP00.A?format=csvdata). This is the daily euro foreign exchange reference-rate series, quoted in **US dollars per one euro**, with unit multiplier zero. These informational reference rates are not executable transaction prices or market closing prices.

The research snapshots cover requested periods 2010–2024 and 2025. Both were retrieved on **23 October 2026**. The separate January 2024 teaching snapshot was retrieved on **8 October 2026**. Exact request URLs, requested ranges, UTC retrieval timestamps and original-byte SHA-256 fingerprints are retained in the companion `data/raw/*.metadata.json` files. These timestamps describe local retrieval records; they do not reconstruct historical data vintages or provide external timestamp certification.

The bundled **raw ECB CSV responses, including their metadata columns, are preserved unchanged**. Their companion retrieval metadata files are also retained unchanged. Those JSON records are project-created provenance records, rather than additional official ECB statistical releases. They document the nine empty holiday rows in the older research response; the original rows remain in the raw CSV. Source fingerprints in the metadata and study summaries allow the retained bytes to be checked for consistency.

Files under `data/processed/`, the return calculations, forecast CSVs, scores and figures are **project transformations and calculations based on ECB statistics**. Processing selects validated quote fields and excludes explicitly flagged holiday placeholders after checking the publication calendar. Subsequent calculations form interval simple returns, squared targets and model forecasts. These derived outputs are not official ECB statistics, and their inclusion does not imply ECB endorsement. The [research report](reports/research_report.md) explains the transformations and limitations.

The ECB’s [policy regarding reuse of ESCB statistics](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/ecb_statistics/governance_and_quality_framework/html/usage_policy.en.html) permits free reuse of publicly available statistics subject to source attribution and preservation of the statistics and metadata; it excludes third-party data from that general permission. Its [copyright conditions](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/services/using-our-site/disclaimer/html/index.en.html) also require accurate reproduction, credit to the ECB, and explicit identification of user modifications, including calculated growth rates. Retain the source credit and the distinction between originals and project-derived outputs when redistributing this material. ECB source content remains governed by the ECB’s published terms.
